Government

National Health Authority

The largest IAM programme in the PITG portfolio, covering about 250K users across a national health platform. Identity lifecycle, access governance and single sign-on were engineered end to end so clinicians and administrators could work from one trusted identity fabric. Custom connectors bridged legacy health systems to Tier-1 IAM controls without disrupting day-to-day care delivery.

Defence

Indian Army UNMS

Identity management for about 140K users in a defence environment with restrictive network conditions. Delivery ran inside air-gapped and highly controlled segments where standard SaaS tooling could not be used. PITG built custom connectors and operational runbooks so identity services stayed available under defence-grade constraints.

BFSI

UOB

SecureMail upgraded from v7.7 to v24.3.1 across Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia. The multi-country rollout was phased so mail flow and encryption controls stayed intact for every market. PITG coordinated platform upgrades, regression checks and cutover windows with local operations teams.

Data security

CelcomDigi

On-premise SDA with format-preserving encryption, tokenisation and REST API integration. Sensitive fields in a Vertica analytics environment were protected while remaining usable for reporting and downstream systems. Format-preserving encryption and tokenisation were wired through REST APIs so applications could adopt controls without a full rewrite.

Healthcare

Synapxe

PII and PHI protection for Singapore health data, with masking and key rotation. Structured data protection kept clinical and demographic fields usable for analytics while reducing exposure risk. Masking, encryption and key-rotation practices were aligned to Singapore healthcare compliance expectations.

SOC

West Bengal State SOC

A sovereign managed security operations centre run from Kolkata for state infrastructure. Monitoring, detection and response services were stood up as a trusted sovereign SOC model. SIEM, SOAR and related controls were operated under local governance so sensitive telemetry stayed within the required boundary.
